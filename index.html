ABOUT US

Experience, dedication,
and a family connection.

Meet the people behind Restore and Roll.

OWNER

Walt Howard

Walt brings more than 30 years of experience to Restore and Roll, including award-winning rehabilitation work at the Hershey hotel in Pennsylvania. His experience spans multiple aspects of a project, with a focus on quality workmanship, careful coordination, and staying within budget. He has also trained and mentored apprentices entering the trade, sharing the practical skills and pride in craftsmanship he has developed throughout his career.

Before starting his own business, Walt served as a regional manager for a million-dollar company, overseeing multiple offices across several states. He brings that leadership experience to Restore and Roll, combining hands-on expertise with a personal commitment to every project.

ADMINISTRATOR

Makye Detwiler

Born and raised in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, Makye manages the administrative side of Restore and Roll while working full time for Southwest Airlines. As the grandson of Walt and Nancy Howard, he brings a personal connection to the business and a commitment to supporting its day-to-day operations.

Outside of work, Makye enjoys fishing, hiking, traveling, and spending time with his cat, Peanut.
